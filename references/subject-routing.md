# Subject Routing and Abstraction Control

Read this file after inspecting the source photo. Select one primary route, one optional secondary route, and one abstraction level.

## Abstraction levels

| Level | Broad marks | Recognition target | Use |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `restrained` | 5–9 | posture or relation only | user asks for more abstraction, quiet scenes, one clear subject |
| `balanced` | 9–14 | relation first, subject on second glance | default |
| `expressive` | 14–22 | strong mass and rhythm | layered architecture, crowds, forceful motion |

Count visible mark groups rather than every break inside one dragged stroke. Keep two to four dark anchors at each level.

## Routes

### Gesture

Use for people and animals. Preserve the spine or torso axis, head direction, limb rhythm, overlap, and one identifying interruption. Merge faces, paws, fingers, fur, folds, and anatomy into broad bands and ivory gaps.

### Mass

Use for trees, mountains, clouds, flowers, and dense natural forms. Preserve weight, spread, density change, major silhouette pressure, and negative-space cuts. Avoid repeated leaf, rock, or petal symbols.

### Rhythm

Use for architecture, bridges, trains, crowds, and repeated structures. Preserve layer count, interval changes, dominant verticals, interrupted horizontals, and scale contrast. Remove windows, ornament, bricks, cables, and mechanical detail unless one feature identifies the subject.

### Path

Use for roads, rivers, rails, shorelines, reflections, wind, and movement. Preserve entry point, exit point, curvature, breaks, convergence, and speed change. Give the continuous color line this route when it acts as the secondary relation.

## Route combinations

- person or animal in motion: `gesture + path`
- solitary object under a landscape: `mass + path`
- landmark with bridge or rails: `rhythm + path`
- person beneath a tree or roof: `mass + gesture`

Do not combine more than two routes.

## Fact-to-mark map

Record two to four rows before generation:

```text
source fact -> retained relation -> abstract mark
```

Example:

```text
large mountain above small car -> scale pressure -> dense descending charcoal mass
isolated car -> pause and smallness -> two low dark marks
ground line -> arrival -> one broken warm line
```

Delete a planned mark when no source fact supports it. Keep the map out of the finished artwork.
